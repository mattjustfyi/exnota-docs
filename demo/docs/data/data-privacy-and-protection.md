# Data Privacy and Protection

## Overview

We prioritize data privacy and protection to ensure compliance with global regulations and to build trust with our users. Our policies and systems are designed to safeguard sensitive information.

## Key Principles

- **Transparency**: Clear communication about data usage.
- **Control**: Users can manage their data preferences.
- **Security**: Robust measures to prevent unauthorized access.

## Compliance Standards

- **GDPR**: General Data Protection Regulation (EU).
- **CCPA**: California Consumer Privacy Act.
- **HIPAA**: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (for healthcare data).

## Data Handling Practices

- **Data Minimization**: Collect only what is necessary.
- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- **Access Control**: Role-based access to sensitive data.

## User Rights

- **Access**: Users can request a copy of their data.
- **Correction**: Users can request corrections to their data.
- **Deletion**: Users can request data deletion.

## Incident Response

In the event of a data breach:

1. Immediate containment and assessment.
2. Notification to affected users and authorities.
3. Detailed post-incident analysis and reporting.
