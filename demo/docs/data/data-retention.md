# Data Retention

## Overview

Our data retention policy ensures that data is stored only as long as necessary to fulfill its purpose, comply with legal requirements, and support business operations.

## Retention Periods

| Data Type            | Retention Period       | Notes                          |
|----------------------|------------------------|--------------------------------|
| User Accounts        | Active + 2 years      | Deleted upon user request.    |
| Transaction Records  | 7 years               | Required for compliance.      |
| Analytics Data       | 1 year                | Aggregated for long-term use. |
| Logs                 | 90 days               | Rotated automatically.        |

## Data Deletion

- **Automated Deletion**: Scheduled processes to remove expired data.
- **User-Initiated Deletion**: Users can request data deletion via their account settings.
- **Secure Deletion**: Data is irreversibly deleted using industry-standard methods.

## Exceptions

Certain data may be retained longer for:

- Legal obligations.
- Fraud prevention.
- Historical analysis (anonymized).

## Policy Updates

Our data retention policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed to reflect changes in regulations or business practices.
